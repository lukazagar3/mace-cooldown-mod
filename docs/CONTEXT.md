# Glossary

- **Mace Cooldown** — A custom cooldown applied specifically to the Mace weapon. When a player strikes another player with a Mace, the Mace is placed on item cooldown (visual grey overlay in the hotbar) for a configured duration, during which it cannot be used. Does not apply when hitting non-player entities.
- **Combat Cooldown** (Combat State) — A state of active combat triggered when a player damages or is damaged by another player. While in this state, the player is considered "in combat" and receives a ticking countdown on their actionbar.
- **Pearl Cooldown** — A visual item cooldown of 5 seconds (100 ticks) applied to Ender Pearls when thrown while the player is in the Combat State. Throwing the pearl does not refresh or extend the Combat Cooldown.
- **Ranked System** — A server-wide ranking system that tracks player kills and deaths, ordering players based on total kills (descending) and using deaths (ascending) as a tie-breaker.
- **Kill Trading Protection** — A mechanism that prevents players from artificially boosting their rank by killing the same player repeatedly. If player A kills player B, another kill on player B by player A within 30 minutes will not increment player A's kill count.
- **Tripwire Hook Duplication** — A bug/exploit in vanilla Minecraft where tripwire hooks drop themselves as items during block updates (such as water flowing or pistons moving) without the block actually being destroyed.
